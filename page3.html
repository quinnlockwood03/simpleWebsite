Our Origin Story

Our story began with something we all share: a personal connection to animals, especially dogs. As co-founders, Taylor Lavis, Quinn Lockwood, and Veena Patel wanted to turn that love into a business where people could feel confident that their pets were in safe, caring hands.

That shared purpose became Paws & Polish. We imagined a welcoming place where every animal would be treated with patience, kindness, and care, and every owner could feel comfortable trusting us with their furry family member. It started with an idea and transformed into a story!

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you only pamper dogs?

In our fictional salon, dogs are our specialty! Cats are welcome to apply for the honorary position of Front Desk Supervisor.

Can my pup choose a bandana?

Absolutely. Our imaginary bandana bar has everything from classic polka dots to “I passed obedience school” energy.

What if my dog is nervous?

Our pretend spa menu includes the Slow & Steady welcome: extra introduction time, a quiet corner, and plenty of encouragement.

Can I actually book an appointment?

This is a fictional student business. You can meet our co-founders and try the demo contact form, but we do not offer real appointments.
